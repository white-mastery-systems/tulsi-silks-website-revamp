2. Why rankings dropped

1

Too many weak pages

About 30 Kanjivaram categories have 0 products or duplicate each other. They compete with the hub and make the site look thin.

2

Broken SEO basics

Temporary 302 redirects, 5 pages with the default site title, product cards showing ₹0, and an FAQ that exists only in hidden JSON-LD.

3

Weak internal links

The hub doesn't link to its sub-pages or to blogs, and blogs don't link back to the hub.

7. How to check your work

  • Redirects: run curl -sI <old-url>. It should show 301 and the correct Location.
  • Page content: open view-source (raw HTML, not DevTools). The title, H1, meta, intro, robots tag, FAQ text and links must all be there.
  • Sitemap: noindex pages and redirect sources must not appear in /sitemap.xml.
  • Links: no internal link should point at a URL that redirects.
  • FAQ: the visible text and the JSON-LD must match word for word. Test with Google's Rich Results Test.
  • Google: within 14 days, URL Inspection on old URLs shows "Page with redirect".
