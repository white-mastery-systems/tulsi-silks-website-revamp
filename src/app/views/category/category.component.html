Max is a furniture and object designer from Barcelona. His work is defined both as design and art, in fact most of the pieces he creates could actually work as sculptures.

What we like about his work is how he plays with the materials. Iron, stone, plaster, marble or wood are some of his favorites. Some of his pieces are self-produced as an exercise of experimentation, although most of them are made in local workshops in Barcelona.

We’ve gathered some limited edition pieces, like his iconic Stabile table in a unique electric blue color or the 3-Planes side table, amongst others. All in exclusiveness for us.
